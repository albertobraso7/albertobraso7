## Alberto Brambila, PhD — CFD & Thermofluids Engineer

CFD models and simulation tools for multiphase flow, heat transfer, rotating machinery and hydrogen systems.
Experience across 37 professional projects in pumps, valves, thermal systems and gas–liquid separation, plus doctoral research in fluid–structure interaction and non-Newtonian hemodynamics (UNAM, 2025).

**Based in Mexico** · open to international relocation
